Our story

North Star started in 2014 with one secondhand oven and a sourdough starter named Polaris.

Founder Maria Delgado baked out of a rented church kitchen and sold loaves from a folding table at the Saturday market. Neighbors kept coming back, and asking for more. Two years later we opened our doors on Maple Street. We're still a small team, we still know most of our regulars by name, and Polaris is still going strong.

How we source

Good bread starts long before the kitchen. We keep our ingredient list short and our suppliers close.

  • Flour stone-milled from wheat and rye grown within 200 miles.
  • Butter, milk, and eggs from two family farms we visit every season.
  • Fruit bought weekly at the farmers market, so our danishes change with what's ripe.
  • No shortcuts: no dough conditioners, no artificial preservatives, nothing we can't pronounce.

Day-old bread goes to the Northside Community Pantry every evening.
